## What We Build

Most AI systems today run inside centralized clouds that pool millions of private lives under commercial incentives while acting across the public internet with standing credentials. We build open technical and institutional alternatives across five connected parts:

1. **Honest Local Sensors & Hardware Attestation:** Wearable and home sensors synchronized locally with explicit uncertainty bounds, silicon attestation, optical data diodes, and passive physical dead-man relays.
2. **Encrypted Local Memory Vault:** Append-only personal memory stored on hardware you control, encrypted strictly for you and your personal assistant.
3. **1:1 Loyal Personal Assistant:** One personal model bound by a statutory duty of loyalty to one living person's wishes, unassisted judgment, and wellbeing.
4. **Two Separate Ledgers & Expiring Capability Leases:** Keeping personal life private at home while attaching short, revocable capability leases and zero-knowledge receipts to outward actions on a dedicated signed network road for agents.
5. **Public Computing & Cooperative Coordination:** Pairing local software synthesis and Universal Basic Compute with cooperative equity shares, capacity-backed mutual credit, and bottom-up community rules.
